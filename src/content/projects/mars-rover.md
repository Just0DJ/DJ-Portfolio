## Project Overview

Multi-year competitive robotics project — Mechanical Design Engineer & Subsystem Lead, Apr 2024 to present, spanning three international competitions with progressive responsibilities. Engineered a modular lightweight chassis using a rocker-bogie kinematic suspension system, validating articulation, ground clearances, and dynamic stability in SolidWorks Motion.

![Mars Rover Society at IRC Goa 2025](../../assets/science-cache-1.png)

## 1. Science Cache System — Autonomous Sample Collection

**Design:** Engineered compact autonomous sample collection system integrating 6-beaker rotating carousel (100ml capacity each) with helical screw drilling actuator and chemical mixing subsystem in a single actuator-minimized assembly.

**CAD Development:** Created comprehensive CAD models in SolidWorks with detailed mechanical drawings, assemblies, and complete Bills of Materials (BOMs) for manufacturing and procurement.

**Prototyping:** Fabricated functional prototype via FDM 3D printing (PLA material) with iterative design modifications. Resolved critical dust-sealing challenges at mechanism interfaces through gasket design and tolerance optimization for field-ready deployment in sandy terrain.

**Validation:** Conducted physical testing including drilling force analysis, carousel indexing accuracy, and sample retention reliability under simulated Mars-analog environmental conditions.

## 2. Modular Rover Chassis — Structural Design & Kinematics

**Architecture:** Engineered lightweight, modular chassis architecture using PVC pipes and 3-way joint connectors optimized for rapid assembly/disassembly, center-of-mass management, and integration with rocker-bogie suspension, differential drive system, and battery mounting platforms.

**Motion Analysis:** Performed comprehensive SolidWorks Motion Analysis to validate suspension kinematics, wheel articulation, and obstacle clearance parameters for uneven terrain navigation including rock climbing and crater traversal scenarios.

**Structural Validation:** Conducted structural load validation studies to ensure chassis integrity under static and dynamic loading conditions including payload capacity and impact resistance.

## 3. System Integration & Field Deployment

**Competition Deployment (IRC 2025 - Goa):** Led onsite team responsibilities including complete rover assembly, subsystem integration testing, preventive maintenance, and real-time troubleshooting of mechanical and electromechanical interfaces.

**Manipulator Integration:** Integrated 6-DoF robotic manipulator arm with chassis structure and science cache system, ensuring proper load transfer, cable management, and operational workspace clearances.

**Cross-functional Coordination:** Coordinated with electronics, software, and controls teams for system validation including power distribution, sensor mounting, and communication system integration.

## 4. Technical Documentation & Management

**Competition Documentation (ERC 2025):** Authored comprehensive competition-grade technical documentation and design reports covering detailed design intent, CAD drawings, engineering calculations, testing protocols, subsystem specifications, validation results, and manufacturing procedures.

**Team Management:** Managed team operations including outreach programs, corporate sponsorship acquisition, team branding design, financial planning, and social media campaigns. Coordinated logistics for remote competition participation.

**Mentorship (IRC 2026):** Contributed to technical documentation refinement and mentored junior team members in mechanical design workflows while continuing team management responsibilities.
