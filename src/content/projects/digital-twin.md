## Project Motivation

Independent systems study architected as a predictive maintenance framework coupling real-time sensor streams with SolidWorks and ANSYS models to evaluate dynamic failure modes.

## Framework Development

**System Architecture:** Architected a predictive maintenance framework coupling real-time sensor streams with SolidWorks and ANSYS models to evaluate dynamic failure modes.

**Anomaly Detection:** Developed Python-based anomaly detection scripts to map vibration and thermal telemetry against baseline operational thresholds.

## Target Applications

- **Industrial Equipment Monitoring:** Real-time tracking of equipment health and performance metrics
- **Automation Optimization:** Identifying opportunities for process improvement and efficiency gains
- **Lifecycle Performance Analysis:** Understanding equipment degradation patterns over time
- **Condition-Based Maintenance:** Predictive maintenance scheduling based on actual equipment condition rather than fixed intervals

## Learning Objectives

Building foundational knowledge in Digital Twin Architecture, IoT Integration, Simulation-Driven Design, Predictive Analytics, and Industry 4.0 Technologies to prepare for future roles in smart manufacturing and automated systems.

## Current Status

Conceptual framework development ongoing. Exploring case studies and industry implementations to understand practical challenges and best practices in digital twin deployment.
