## Project Overview

Independent research project developing a 4-layer multifunctional smart window assembly for energy-efficient building applications and intelligent infrastructure automation.

## System Architecture

**Layer 1 — Fiberglass Substrate:** Structural support layer providing mechanical strength and dimensional stability.

**Layer 2 — Electrochromic Film:** Variable light transmission control allowing dynamic adjustment of visible light and heat passage.

**Layer 3 — Transparent Perovskite Solar Panel:** Energy harvesting layer converting sunlight to electrical power while maintaining optical transparency.

**Layer 4 — Transparent LED Display:** Information overlay capability for displaying relevant data without obstructing view.

## Technical Development

**Mechanical Design:** Studied mechanical stacking configurations and structural bonding techniques for laminated assemblies. Designed custom 3D-printed housing enclosure for prototype assembly.

**Performance Analysis:** Analyzed optical performance characteristics including transmittance and haze measurements. Performed basic power generation calculations and system feasibility analysis.

**Integration Challenges:** Explored electrical interconnections between layers, thermal management of embedded electronics, and optical interference minimization.

## Project Outcomes

Validated proof-of-concept design under budget constraints (material sourcing limitations, component availability). Secured faculty approval for prototype demonstration showcasing integration of multifunctional building envelope technologies.

## Applications

- Smart buildings with dynamic light and heat control
- Automotive glazing with integrated displays
- Energy-efficient infrastructure with on-site power generation
