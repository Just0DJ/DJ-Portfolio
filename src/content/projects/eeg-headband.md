## Project Overview

Iterated three physical prototyping generations (V1–V3) of an ergonomic closed-loop headband designed for continuous cognitive fatigue tracking, covering the full loop from sensor contact mechanics to structural validation.

## Mechanical Design

**Sensor Contact:** Designed snap-fit sensor retaining brackets and lightweight housings to maintain continuous skin-electrode contact while distributing pressure evenly across the wearer's head.

**Ergonomics:** Balanced clamping force against long-duration wear comfort, ensuring stable electrode contact without pressure points during extended sessions.

## Structural Validation

Evaluated critical stress concentrations and compliance using ANSYS structural simulations to prevent plastic deformation during repeated donning cycles.

## Prototyping

Three generations of FDM-printed housings and brackets, each iteration tightening tolerance stack-ups and improving snap-fit retention reliability.
