## Team Role

Mechanical Design Engineer & Subsystem Lead on the competition rover team (Apr 2024 – Present), spanning European Rover Challenge Remote 2025, IRC 2025 (Goa), and IRC 2026 (Udupi).

![Science Cache System](../../assets/science-cache-1.png)

## Key Contributions

**Chassis:** Engineered a modular lightweight chassis using a rocker-bogie kinematic suspension system, validating articulation, ground clearances, and dynamic stability in SolidWorks Motion.

**Science Cache:** Designed an autonomous science cache module featuring a 6-beaker indexing carousel, helical core-drill actuator, and dynamic dust-sealed chamber for sample extraction.

**Manipulator:** Integrated a 6-DoF robotic manipulator, executing structural load-path verification and field maintenance at IRC 2025 (Goa) and IRC 2026 (Udupi).

**Results:** Ranked 4th globally at ERC Remote 2025, 10th globally at IRC 2026, and 16th globally at IRC 2025.

## Skills Developed

SolidWorks Motion, Rocker-Bogie Kinematics, Mechanism Design, System Integration, Field Maintenance, Competition Engineering
