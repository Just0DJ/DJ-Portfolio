## Research Context

Under Dr. Raja, HOD of Mechanical Engineering, IIITDM Kancheepuram. Academic research project focused on lithium-ion battery thermal management for electric vehicles.

## Research Activities

**Thermal Analysis:** Executed transient thermal FEA simulations in ANSYS Thermal alongside calibrated infrared thermography to model heat dissipation patterns across lithium-ion cell packs under variable C-rate discharge loads.

**Packaging & Fixturing:** Designed physical packaging layouts, conveyor mounts, and cell-retention enclosures for battery module assemblies.

**Prototyping:** Fabricated physical test modules for experimental heat dissipation validation, including assembly procedures and quality validation protocols.

## Research Contributions

- Characterized temperature profiles under multiple load scenarios
- Validated thermal management effectiveness through simulation and physical testing
- Contributed to research paper on battery safety systems
